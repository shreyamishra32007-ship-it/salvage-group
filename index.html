About Salvage Group

Salvage Group is a professionally managed organization specializing in:

  • ✔ Pest Control Services
  • ✔ Methyl Bromide (MBR) Fumigation
  • ✔ Heat Treatment (HT) Solutions for Wooden Packaging Materials & Pallets

Established with a strong commitment to Quality, Safety & Customer Satisfaction, we provide reliable and effective services to industries, exporters, warehouses, logistics companies, and commercial establishments.

With trained professionals, modern equipment, approved chemicals, and strict compliance with international standards, we ensure safe and efficient service delivery.
